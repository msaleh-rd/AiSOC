# Intelligence SOC Trademark and Brand Policy

Intelligence SOC (formerly branded "AiSOC" — the project's GitHub repository,
`beenuar/AiSOC`, and its live domain, `tryaisoc.com`, retain the historical
name for continuity) is free under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The **name and
logo** are not part of that grant. This is deliberate: MIT lets anyone fork
the code (that is the point), but a funded competitor should not be able to
fork it, outspend the community on marketing, and pass their product off as
"Intelligence SOC."

## What you may do without permission

- Use, fork, modify, and redistribute the source under the MIT License.
- State truthfully that your product is "built on Intelligence SOC," "powered
  by Intelligence SOC," or "a fork of Intelligence SOC," provided it is clear
  you are not the official project.
- Use the Intelligence SOC name in blog posts, talks, comparisons, and
  documentation that refer to the project.

## What requires permission

- Naming a fork, product, service, or company "Intelligence SOC" (or a
  confusingly similar name).
- Using the Intelligence SOC name or logo as your own product/service
  branding, or in a way that implies official endorsement, affiliation, or
  that your distribution is the canonical one.
- Using the Intelligence SOC logo in modified form, or on merchandise.

## Distributing a modified version

If you distribute a modified build, please rename it so users are not misled
about what they are running and who supports it. You must keep the MIT
`LICENSE` and copyright notices intact, but the product name should be yours.

## Hosted / managed offerings

Offering a hosted or managed service is permitted by the MIT License, but you
may not market it under the Intelligence SOC name or imply it is the official
hosted Intelligence SOC without permission.

## Requests

To request permission for a use not covered above, open a discussion or
contact the maintainers via the channels in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md). We aim
to be permissive for community, educational, and non-deceptive uses.

This policy may evolve as the project's governance matures (see
[`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md) — governance phase). It does not restrict any
right granted by the MIT License to the code itself.
